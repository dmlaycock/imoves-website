Advice Centre for Teachers

These free resources have been developed to support primary school teachers who are working with children aged 4 to 11 years old, and looking to create fun, active learning environments where children can flourish, whilst maintaining good physical, emotional and social wellness.

Use these resources as often as you need them, share with your colleagues and keep checking back for new additions.

Click on the links in each section to go to the resources...

Teaching Physical Education with confidence...

How can you choose the right PE programme for your school?  These three short videos take you through 3 outdated approaches to PE; the seven secret ingredients every PE curriculum should include; and finally what Ofsted are really looking for from you. 

PE Curriculum Builder - this free webinar and guide has been developed to support a PE lead with PE planning for the entire school! Includes curriculum map, an example of a learning journey, progression of skills template for gymnastics and loads more useful info. 

How can you build up confidence in teaching what many see as the most challenging area of the whole primary school curriculum - physical education.  That’s why we’ve produced this FREE guide to help teachers build confidence and deliver OUTSTANDING PE lessons.

Supporting children's Mental Wellbeing...

Mental wellbeing can be broken down into 3 aspects; emotional wellbeing, social wellbeing, and physical wellbeing.  Click on the links to find a short video and loads of useful hints and tips on each aspect to use in your class tomorrow.

Helping children manage their emotions, or how to manage their worries or concerns can seem daunting.  Click on the links to use some simple techniques with your your children we've seen work time and time again.  

Managing anxiety and building a growth mindset so they can be resilient and more confident are important skills for any child to learn.  Click on the links to find out how you can support your children develop them.

Build Core Strength using Pilates

Pilates is an amazing way to develop a child's core strength as well as improve their ABC's (agility, balance and coordination), some of the basic elements that every good PE curriculum should include. The movements can be taught safely and highly effectively in your classroom with the right resources.

This 30-minute webinar will provide the tips and guidance you need to confidently deliver Pilates activities with your children.
